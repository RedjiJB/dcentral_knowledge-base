## B2B Thesis

The residential sod market in Ottawa is seasonal, weather-dependent, geographically scattered, and capped by single-homeowner contract sizes. A single residential job generates $800–$3,500. A single B2B contract generates $15,000–$2,000,000 per year. The ceiling is not a ceiling — it is a different market altogether.

But the B2B case for D-Central is not simply "larger contracts." The B2B case is that **D-Central's cooperative architecture makes promises that no traditional Ottawa landscaping company can make and keep:**

- Real-time portfolio visibility across dozens of properties (PMCs cannot get this)
- Cryptographically verifiable crew certifications (safety-critical B2B clients demand this)
- Professional-grade aerial survey reports as a standard deliverable (competitors charge extra or don't offer it)
- Transparent, auditable pricing (procurement teams require this)
- Cooperative social value multiplier (ESG programs reward it)
- Geographic zone efficiency (competitive bids without margin erosion)

These are not marketing claims. They are structural capabilities built into the D-Central stack that no competitor has — because no competitor built the stack.

The B2B expansion does not displace the residential operation. It uses the same crew, the same machines, the same cooperative infrastructure, and the same zone clustering logic — at a different revenue scale.

## The Seven B2B Client Segments

### Segment 1: Property Management Companies (PMCs)

**Who they are:** Companies managing portfolios of residential, commercial, and mixed-use properties on behalf of owners. They outsource grounds maintenance entirely.

**Ottawa targets:** Minto Properties, CLV Group, Taggart Corporation, DCR Phoenix, Ashcroft Homes, Real Star Property Management, Paramount Properties, Osgoode Properties.

**What they need:**
- A single vendor for multiple properties (simplified vendor management)
- Consistent service quality across portfolio
- Real-time documentation (photos, completion logs, condition reports)
- Single monthly invoice with per-property breakdown
- Escalation path when something goes wrong

**D-Central delivers:**
- D-PORTFOLIO: one client account, all properties, all jobs, one dashboard
- D-CLIENT B2B portal: PMC sees every property's job status in real time
- D-REPORT: monthly condition reports per property, photo-documented
- D-SCOUT: aerial surveys on any property on request, stored in D-RECORD
- D-INVOICE B2B: single invoice, line-itemized by property, digital delivery
- D-SLA: response time and completion targets tracked automatically

**Revenue model:** Annual grounds maintenance contract per property. Average $18,000–$45,000/property/year depending on lot size and service scope.

**Penetration strategy:** Target PMCs with Ottawa portfolios of 10–50 properties first. A 20-property PMC contract at $25K average = $500K/year with a single client relationship.

### B2B-Only Services

**Hydroseeding:**
Large-scale revegetation faster and cheaper than sod at scale. Uses a slurry of seed, mulch, water, and fertilizer applied by spray. Required for:
- Construction site revegetation (erosion control regulations)
- Highway and municipal right-of-way restoration
- Large commercial properties (fields, slopes, transition zones)
- Revenue: $0.08–$0.25/sq ft — far higher margins than sod at B2B volumes

**Erosion Control and Stormwater Management:**
Ottawa requires erosion control compliance on all construction sites. This is a regulatory need, not discretionary.
- Silt fencing, sediment traps, temporary seeding
- Ongoing compliance monitoring (drone inspection capability is a differentiator)
- Post-construction restoration

**Snow and Ice Management:**
The natural winter contract for existing grounds maintenance clients. Bundles with summer grounds work for year-round revenue.
- Plowing, salting, sanding, sidewalk clearing
- Converts seasonal revenue to year-round revenue
- Existing PMC and CRE clients → automatic winter contract conversation
- Revenue: $15,000–$80,000/property/winter depending on lot size and service level

**Sports Turf Installation and Renovation:**
Specialty service requiring specific knowledge. High value, long-term relationships.

**Irrigation Installation, Service, and Management:**
Commercial properties need irrigation. Sod installations without irrigation are a poor investment. Offering irrigation services as a package:
- Design and installation
- Seasonal startup/shutdown
- Smart controller programming
- Leak detection and repair

**Drone Services (D-AERIAL — see Section 5):**
Standalone commercial service line using existing drone infrastructure.

## Eight New B2B Systems

### B2B-1: D-CONTRACT — Contract Lifecycle Management

B2B relationships are governed by contracts. D-CONTRACT manages the full lifecycle.

**Contract types:**
- Master Service Agreement (MSA): framework governing the relationship
- Statement of Work (SOW): specific scope, timeline, deliverables per project
- Annual Grounds Maintenance Agreement: recurring services, price schedule
- Change Order: scope modifications, approved and tracked

**D-CONTRACT features:**
- Contract templates for each service type and client segment
- Digital signature (DocuSign-compatible or D-ID cryptographic signature)
- Milestone and renewal alerts
- Change order workflow (request → estimate → approve → D-JOB update)
- Contract performance dashboard: SLA compliance, budget vs. actual, renewals pending
- Integration with D-TREASURY for payment terms and invoice scheduling

**Pricing structure for MSAs:**
B2B pricing is structured differently from residential quotes:
- Unit rates (per sq ft, per linear ft, per hour) established in MSA
- SOW specifies quantities → D-SCOPE calculates total
- Price escalation clauses (CPI-linked for multi-year contracts)
- Volume discount tiers locked into MSA

### B2B-4: D-TENDER — Procurement Intelligence System

Government and institutional clients issue tenders on public portals. Most small landscaping companies miss them or respond poorly. D-TENDER automates the front end.

**Monitoring sources:**
- MERX (Canadian Government procurement)
- Ontario Tenders Portal
- City of Ottawa procurement portal
- National Capital Commission (NCC) vendor opportunities
- OCDSB, OCSB, uOttawa, Carleton procurement portals
- CanadaBuys (federal procurement)
- Bids & Tenders (Ontario municipalities)

**D-TENDER workflow:**
1. Daily scrape of monitored portals for landscaping, grounds maintenance, sod, turf, revegetation, erosion control keywords
2. Relevant tenders flagged and summarized in D-MESH notification
3. Owner / Growth Circle reviews: bid Y/N / defer
4. If bid: D-TENDER generates base proposal using:
   - D-SCOUT data (if the property is in D-RECORD)
   - D-SCOPE material takeoff from available survey data
   - D-QUOTE unit rates
   - Cooperative social value statement (auto-generated)
   - D-TRAIN credential package (all relevant certifications, WSIB, insurance)
5. Proposal reviewed by Finance Circle for margin approval
6. Submitted through portal or email

**Cooperative social value statement:**
Auto-generated document for every bid that articulates:
- Worker-owned cooperative structure
- Local Ottawa employment
- Crew certification and training investment
- Environmental stewardship metrics (D-CARBON data)
- Indigenous land stewardship commitment
- Community reinvestment through D-CRED and profit sharing

This document is worth 10–30% of a weighted bid score in social-value-weighted procurement. It is already true — it just needs to be written and maintained.

**Bid tracking:**
- All submitted bids tracked with status
- Win/loss analysis (was price the issue? qualifications? capacity?)
- Win rate target: 35% of submitted bids
- Post-bid debrief requests for losses

### B2B-5: D-SLA — Service Level Agreement Engine

B2B clients write SLAs into their contracts. A broken SLA is a contract breach. D-SLA makes compliance automatic.

**Standard SLA metrics for grounds maintenance:**
| Metric | Standard Target | Premium Target |
|--------|---------------|----------------|
| Response to service call | < 4 hours | < 2 hours |
| Emergency response (storm damage) | < 24 hours | < 8 hours |
| Scheduled service completion (vs. agreed date) | 95% on-time | 99% on-time |
| Report delivery (monthly) | By 5th of next month | By 3rd |
| Escalation response (client complaint) | < 1 hour | < 30 min |

**D-SLA tracking:**
- Every D-JOB has a scheduled date → D-SLA monitors completion vs. date
- Every D-LEADS (service call) has a response time tracked from receipt
- D-WEATHER integration: weather-caused delays automatically documented (force majeure evidence)
- SLA dashboard per client: current compliance rate, trend, any breaches
- Breach alert: fires before the breach (predictive: "this job is at risk of missing SLA")
- Monthly SLA compliance report auto-generated for D-REPORT

**SLA breach protocol:**
1. D-SLA fires alert 4 hours before predicted breach
2. Site lead notified, escalation option
3. If breach occurs: automatic incident record created
4. Account manager notified → client communication within 30 min
5. Root cause logged in D-JOB incident record
6. Remediation scheduled automatically
7. Client receives incident summary via D-CLIENT portal

### B2B-6: D-SCOPE — Large Project Scope Management

B2B projects are complex. A development phase with 80 lots, a commercial park with 15 zones, a university campus with seasonal programs — these require scope management that D-JOB alone doesn't provide.

**D-SCOPE functions:**
- **Bill of quantities (BoQ):** From D-SCOUT aerial measurement data, generates precise material quantities per zone or lot
- **Phase planning:** Large projects broken into phases with milestones, dependencies, and resource requirements
- **Material takeoff:** Total sod pallets, cubic yards of topsoil, linear feet of edging across entire scope
- **Resource loading:** How many crew-days, machine-hours, and equipment moves does this scope require?
- **Schedule integration:** Phases exported to D-ROUTE for scheduling, with D-SUPPLY orders pre-positioned for each phase start
- **Change order tracking:** Original scope vs. current scope, all changes authorized and costed

**BoQ from D-SCOUT:**

When D-SCOUT surveys a development or commercial property:
1. Orthophoto segmented by zone/lot
2. Each zone's landscapeable area measured from aerial data (±2% accuracy)
3. Material quantities calculated automatically:
   - Sod: area ÷ 9 = pallets (standard pallet covers 9 sq ft... actually a pallet covers ~50 sq yd = 450 sq ft)
   - Topsoil: area × depth (0.1m) = cubic meters
   - Edging: perimeter measurement from orthophoto tracing
4. BoQ exported to D-QUOTE for pricing
5. BoQ used in D-TENDER proposal document

This eliminates manual estimation errors — the single largest source of margin erosion in landscape contracts.

### B2B-7: D-INVOICE — B2B Financial Operations

Residential jobs are paid at completion. B2B contracts have net-30/60/90 terms, progress billing, holdbacks, and complex payment structures. D-INVOICE handles this.

**B2B billing structures:**
- **Recurring maintenance:** Monthly flat fee, auto-invoice on the 1st
- **Project milestone billing:** % of contract at defined milestones (30% mobilization, 30% mid-project, 40% completion)
- **Unit-price billing:** Monthly invoice based on units completed × unit rate (sq ft sod installed, hours of maintenance)
- **Retainer + time-and-materials:** Fixed monthly retainer + T&M for additional scope

**D-INVOICE features:**
- Auto-generated from D-JOB completion data + D-CONTRACT terms
- Electronic delivery (email PDF + portal link)
- HST calculation and compliance
- EDI/XML export for large corporate clients' AP systems
- Payment tracking: days outstanding, aging report
- Automated follow-up: 5 days before due, on due date, 5 days after
- Integration with D-TREASURY: payment receipt updates cooperative ledger automatically
- Holdback management: construction contracts typically hold 10% until substantial completion

## The Meta-Product: D-Central Platform as B2B Product

This is the highest-leverage B2B play in the entire architecture.

Sod Boys built a field cooperative platform that solves operational problems no existing software solves for field service cooperatives. Other landscaping companies, grounds maintenance firms, and field service cooperatives have the same problems. They cannot build this themselves. They will pay to access it.

**D-PLATFORM: Cooperative Field Operations SaaS**

D-Central is licensed to other field service companies as a monthly SaaS product. The licensee gets:
- All 38+ D-Central systems deployed in their cooperative
- Onboarding support (setup, configuration, crew training)
- D-SCOUT for their own zone pricing campaigns
- D-TRAIN certification pathway
- D-FRANCHISE network access (equipment sharing, purchasing pool)

**Pricing:**
- Cooperative license: $400/month (companies under 20 crew)
- Growth license: $900/month (20–50 crew)
- Enterprise license: $2,200/month (50+ crew)
- Implementation fee: $3,000–$8,000 (one-time)

**Target licensees:** Grounds maintenance cooperatives, field service companies in landscaping, snow removal, agriculture, construction, environmental services — nationally and internationally.

**10 licensees at $700/month average = $84,000/year in platform revenue — passive, not dependent on crew deployment.**

**Why this works as a business:**
- The platform is already built for internal use
- Licensing is pure margin (server costs don't scale with licensees until 50+)
- Network effects: more licensees → larger equipment sharing pool → better purchasing leverage → everyone benefits
- D-FRANCHISE becomes the D-Central cooperative network

This is the transition from a cooperative landscaping company to a **cooperative infrastructure company that also lays sod.**

## B2B Acquisition Architecture

### The D-Central B2B Funnel

Traditional landscaping companies get B2B clients through cold calls, word of mouth, and accidental referrals. D-Central gets B2B clients through intelligence-led outreach.

**Step 1 — D-SCOUT Commercial Scan:**
D-SCOUT already flies residential neighborhoods. When the survey radius includes commercial properties:
- D-RECORD creates entries for all commercial parcels
- Condition score applied to visible grounds
- Property type classified from Ottawa assessment data (residential / commercial / institutional / industrial)
- High-opportunity commercial leads flagged automatically

**Step 2 — D-TENDER Intelligence:**
D-TENDER monitors all procurement portals. When a tender is issued, the cooperative has often already surveyed the property. The bid goes in with actual aerial data, not estimations.

**Step 3 — D-REPORT as a Sales Tool:**
Offer a free Preliminary Property Report to any target B2B client:
- Free D-AERIAL survey of their primary property
- Professional 8-page report: aerial orthophoto, condition assessment, recommended interventions, indicative pricing
- No obligation, no pressure
- The report demonstrates capabilities that competitors cannot replicate
- Most PMCs have never seen their portfolio from the air

**Step 4 — D-PARTNER B2B:**
Formalize referral relationships with:
- Commercial real estate brokers (new leases → new grounds management needs)
- General contractors (new construction completion → sod/landscaping contract)
- Property inspectors (identify grounds condition issues → refer remediation)
- Facility management trade associations (BOMA Ottawa, IFMA Ottawa Chapter)

**Step 5 — D-CONTENT B2B:**
Commercial work generates premium content:
- Drone footage of development sod installation at scale
- Before/after of commercial property restoration
- The cooperative story for corporate ESG newsletters
- This content attracts B2B clients who self-select on values alignment

## B2B Financial Architecture

### B2B pricing is structurally different

| Factor | Residential | B2B |
|--------|------------|-----|
| Contract size | $800–$3,500 | $15,000–$2,000,000 |
| Payment terms | Due at completion | Net 30–60 |
| Revenue recognition | Per job | Per milestone or monthly |
| Contract length | One-time or seasonal | 1–3 year |
| Renewal rate | 35–55% | 70–85% |
| Margin profile | 28–38% | 22–32% (lower but volume) |
| Cash flow pattern | Immediate | Delayed but predictable |

### Working capital implication

B2B introduces a cash flow gap: large jobs start before payment arrives. A $280,000 development contract with net-30 billing on milestones means the cooperative is fronting materials and labor costs for 30–60 days before receiving payment.

**D-TREASURY B2B adaptation:**
- Separate B2B working capital reserve (20% of B2B contract value pre-funded)
- Progress billing scheduled to match material purchase timing (not project milestones)
- Supplier payment terms negotiated to net-30 (matching client terms)
- Line of credit relationship with a Canadian cooperative bank (Desjardins, cooperative-aligned)

### D-BOND: Cooperative Capital Bonds

When the cooperative needs to scale capacity for a large B2B contract (new equipment, crew expansion), it issues D-Bonds to existing members:
- Members invest D-Credits or cash into the cooperative
- Bonds mature over 2–3 years at a fixed return (5–8%)
- Bonds fund equipment purchases or working capital
- Members earn return on their cooperative investment
- No external bank required for growth capital

## Ottawa B2B Competitive Landscape

Current major commercial grounds maintenance operators in Ottawa:

**Clintar Commercial Outdoor Services:** National franchise, strong commercial presence, no cooperative structure, no drone capability, traditional estimating.

**GreenFirst Services:** Regional, grounds maintenance focus, institutional clients.

**Nutri-Lawn Ottawa:** Lawn care focus, not full grounds maintenance.

**BrightPath Landscaping:** Residential-focused, limited commercial.

**None of the above offers:**
- Worker cooperative structure (ESG scoring in procurement)
- Aerial survey reports as a standard service deliverable
- Real-time client portfolio portal
- Zone pricing for development-scale projects
- Verifiable digital crew credentials

**The competitive moat is the stack.** A traditional landscaping company cannot replicate D-Central in 6 months. They would have to build every system from scratch, change their operational model, restructure their employment relationships, and acquire drone capability. By the time any competitor attempts to match any single feature, D-Central has moved three layers further.
