## 1. Executive Vision & Overview

### 1.1 Mission Statement

To empower Ottawa's Black business community through ownership and operation of decentralized digital infrastructure, creating sustainable wealth, employment opportunities, and technological sovereignty while serving as a global model for community-driven economic development.

### 1.2 Core Value Propositions

#### For Individual Businesses
- Generate $2,000-10,000/month in additional revenue
- Reduce operational costs by 30-50%
- Access to enterprise-grade technology at community prices
- Build transferable tech skills and certifications
- Create generational wealth through infrastructure ownership

#### For the Community
- 100+ micro-enterprises created within 18 months
- 2,000+ direct jobs, 5,000+ indirect employment
- $50M+ in community-owned infrastructure value
- Establish Black-owned alternatives to traditional monopolies
- Create sustainable career paths in emerging technologies

#### For Society
- Bridge the digital divide in underserved communities
- Model for inclusive economic development
- Reduced environmental impact through shared infrastructure
- Enhanced community resilience and emergency preparedness
- Innovation in decentralized governance models

### 1.3 Strategic Objectives

#### Year 1 Goals
- Deploy infrastructure to 100 Black-owned businesses
- Create 50 micro-enterprises

- Establish governance structure and community fund

#### Year 5 Vision
- 1,000+ businesses in network
