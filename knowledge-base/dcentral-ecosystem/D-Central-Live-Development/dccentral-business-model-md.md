# D Central Condo - Crowdfunded & Crowdsourced Business Model

## Executive Summary

D Central Condo represents a paradigm shift in residential living - a crowd-powered, blockchain-enabled smart building ecosystem that transforms residents from passive tenants into active stakeholders. At its core is a revolutionary **App Store for Building Services**, where developers worldwide can create specialized apps that residents use for everything from maintenance to wellness services.

By leveraging crowdfunding for capital and crowdsourcing for operations, we create a self-sustaining economic model that reduces costs by 30-50%, generates new income streams, and builds stronger communities. The platform enables any developer to build and monetize service applications, creating an open innovation ecosystem that benefits all stakeholders.

## 4. Revenue Model

### 4.1 Primary Revenue Streams

**1. Internet Service Margins (35% of revenue)**
- Wholesale bandwidth cost: $20/unit
- Resident price: $40/unit
- Margin: $20/unit × 500 units = $10,000/month

**2. App Store Transaction Fees (40% of revenue)**
- Platform fee: 5-10% of all transactions
- Premium app listings: $100-500/month
- Developer account fees: $50/year
- API access subscriptions: $500/month
- Projected GMV: $250,000/month = $12,500-25,000 fees

**3. Premium Services (15% of revenue)**
- Enterprise internet: $200/month (20 units)
- Smart home packages: $50/month (100 units)
- Priority support: $30/month (50 units)
- Developer tools: $100/month (25 developers)

**4. Data & Analytics (10% of revenue)**
- Anonymized usage patterns
- App performance metrics
- Market research panels
- Developer insights dashboard

**App Economy Metrics:**
- Average app transaction: $50-200

- Developer earnings: $500-50,000/month
