## Objection Handling Guide

### "It sounds too good to be true"
**Response**: "That's because traditional infrastructure is designed to extract value, not share it. Here are 5 businesses already saving $X per month. Let's visit one tomorrow."

### "We don't have the investment capital"
**Response**: "We offer 5 investment models including revenue-share with $0 upfront. You can even contribute equipment you already own."

### "What if the network fails?"
**Response**: "The mesh is more reliable than traditional internet because it has no single point of failure. If one node fails, traffic routes around it automatically."

### "We're not tech-savvy"
**Response**: "The system is designed for busy business owners. Installation takes 4 hours, training takes 2 hours, and support is 24/7."

### "Our current vendors won't like this"
**Response**: "You'll still need some traditional services. But wouldn't you rather have negotiating power with multiple options?"
