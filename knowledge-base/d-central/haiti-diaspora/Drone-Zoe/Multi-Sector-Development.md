```
    ├── Technical Support

```

**Hub Types and Sizing:**
1. **Micro Hub** (100-500 people served)
   - Basic services in limited space
   - Often in existing buildings or small new construction
   - Staffed part-time by community members
   - Investment: $10,000-50,000

2. **Community Hub** (500-2,000 people served)
   - Comprehensive services with dedicated facility
   - Purpose-built or significantly renovated space
   - Full-time staff with multiple service providers
   - Investment: $50,000-200,000

3. **Regional Hub** (2,000-10,000 people served)
   - Full service center with specialized capabilities
   - Multi-building complex with outdoor space
   - Professional staff across all services
   - Investment: $200,000-1,000,000

**Implementation Approach:**
- Begin with existing community spaces where possible
- Implement core infrastructure for essential services
- Add services incrementally as capacity develops
- Create modular designs for expansion
- Develop specialized functions based on community needs

## 14. Conclusion: A Unified Vision for Haiti's Development

This comprehensive framework presents a transformative approach to development in Haiti by integrating multiple sectors into a cohesive, democratized system. By building on the principles established in the Zoe Drone Platform and expanding them across all essential infrastructure and services, this model creates a pathway to community-owned, sustainable development.

The key elements that make this approach powerful include:

1. **Complete Integration** - Each sector strengthens others through thoughtful connections
2. **Democratic Ownership** - Communities control their development through participatory governance
3. **Distributed Design** - Resilient, decentralized systems that cannot easily fail
4. **Economic Inclusion** - Micro-investment and participation opportunities for everyone
5. **Cultural Alignment** - Systems designed to respect and enhance Haitian culture and values
6. **Technical Innovation** - Appropriate technology deployed with community ownership
7. **Diaspora Connection** - Meaningful engagement of Haiti's global community
8. **Environmental Regeneration** - Development that heals rather than harms ecosystems
9. **Self-Determination** - Independence from external control and exploitation
10. **Scalable Replication** - A model that can grow organically across Haiti and beyond

Through this unified framework, Haiti can chart a new path of development that builds on the strength, resilience, and creativity of its people while creating sustainable prosperity and wellbeing for all.
