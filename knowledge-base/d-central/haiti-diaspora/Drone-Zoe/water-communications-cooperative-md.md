## 3. TARGET MARKETS & REVENUE STREAMS

**1. Clean Water Supply & Distribution (524M - 1.31B HTG annual potential)**
- **Target Clients**: Residential customers, businesses, institutions, government
- **Services**: Water treatment, distribution, quality testing, emergency supply
- **Pricing**: 6.5-26.2 HTG per gallon, 65.5K-262K HTG monthly service contracts

**2. Internet Service Provider (393M - 1.18B HTG annual potential)**
- **Target Clients**: Homes, businesses, schools, hospitals, government offices

- **Pricing**: 2.62K-13.1K HTG monthly residential, 13.1K-131K HTG monthly business
