# OpenVision Platform

**Date:** November 2025

## Executive Overview

OpenVision Platform is a **fully open-source, enterprise-grade video surveillance and IoT integration ecosystem** designed to eliminate vendor lock-in, reduce total cost of ownership by 30-50%, and provide unlimited customization capabilities for security and operational intelligence.

### The Opportunity

The global video surveillance market is valued at $50B+ annually, dominated by proprietary vendors (Genetec, Milestone, Axis, Verkada) who charge premium licensing fees while limiting customization and creating dependency. Organizations face:

- **High licensing costs**: $200-400 per camera + 20% annual maintenance
- **Vendor lock-in**: Proprietary formats and APIs prevent switching
- **Limited customization**: Closed systems can't adapt to unique needs
- **Data control concerns**: Cloud-dependent solutions raise security/privacy issues
- **Integration complexity**: Siloed systems don't communicate

### The Solution

OpenVision Platform delivers a **complete open-source alternative** that provides:

✅ **Zero licensing costs** - 100% Apache 2.0 licensed  
✅ **Complete customization** - Modular plugin architecture  
✅ **Edge-first design** - Process locally, reduce cloud dependency  
✅ **Open hardware support** - Run on commodity/open hardware  
✅ **Production-ready** - Enterprise HA, security, monitoring included  
✅ **Own your data** - No cloud lock-in, complete control

## Business Model Options

### 1. Enterprise Self-Hosted
- **Model**: Sell professional services, support contracts
- **Revenue**: Implementation fees + annual support
- **Target**: Fortune 500, government, critical infrastructure
- **Margin**: 40-60% (services-based)

### 2. Managed Platform
- **Model**: Installation + managed hosting/monitoring
- **Revenue**: Setup fee + monthly management ($5-15/camera/month)
- **Target**: Mid-market businesses (100-500 cameras)
- **Margin**: 50-70% (recurring SaaS economics)

### 3. Multi-Tenant SaaS
- **Model**: Cloud-hosted, subscription-based
- **Revenue**: $9.99-49.99/month tiered pricing
- **Target**: SMB and residential (1-50 cameras)
- **Margin**: 70-80% (high-margin SaaS)

### 4. Open Core
- **Model**: Free community edition + paid enterprise features
- **Revenue**: Enterprise licenses + support
- **Target**: All segments
- **Margin**: 60-80% (software licensing)

**Recommended:** Hybrid approach - Open core foundation with managed services and SaaS options
