# DC-FRACTAL-001 — Fractal Cell Architecture (The Franchise Co-op Model)

**Registry ID:** DC-FRACTAL-001
**Status:** Design Complete — structurally validated only at N=2 cells (see §7)
**Owner:** Company Zero as Protocol Steward
**Related:** DC-TPL-000, DC-MOGUL-001, DC-GOV-002, DC-LIC-001, DC-SWARM-001, DC-LKB-001…003, DC-SCALE-001, Cooperative Transparency Stack, Sovereign DAO OS

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the unit of replication for the entire venture layer of the D-Central ecosystem — the cell — and the recursive structure by which cells compose into clusters, regions, and the ecosystem commons. The model behaves like a franchise where it should (standardization, shared brand infrastructure, quality enforcement, royalty) and like a cooperative where franchising fails people (member ownership, bidirectional improvement flow, commons governance, graduation instead of buyout). It applies the cellular-division and capability-inheritance growth mechanics of DC-SWARM-001 to businesses.

## 2. Cell anatomy

A cell is a venture instance with five mandatory properties. It **runs a DC-TPL template** at a recorded version. It **hosts D-Central infrastructure** — nodes, mesh presence, and sensing appropriate to its template's §2.9. It **wraps in DAO governance** for its community-facing operations per its template's §2.8, with regulated operations excluded per the Regulated Entity Pattern. It **banks through Lakou vaults** — payments, payroll, dues, reserves, and contributions all on protocol rails. It **publishes through CTS** — open books to its members and standardized metrics to the commons.

A cell without all five properties is an ordinary business, not a D-Central cell, whatever template it copied.

## 3. The recursion

The same governance schema repeats at every scale; only membership and scope change.

**Layer 0 — Venture cell.** A gym, an agency branch, an installation crew, a venue. Members: its customers/members and staff. Governs: programming, pricing tiers, community reinvestment.

**Layer 1 — Local cluster.** All cells in a city or district. Shares: a NOC/SOC, agent-cabinet back-office, a cluster vault, cross-cell staffing, and procurement volume. Governs: shared-service budgets, cluster-level launches, inter-cell dispute resolution.

**Layer 2 — Regional assembly.** Clusters across a province or corridor. Governs: regional capital allocation, regulated-entity principals' appointments (ratification), template localization for jurisdiction differences.

**Layer 3 — Ecosystem commons.** All regions plus the protocol steward. Governs: the DC-TPL registry, the Template Standard itself, commons royalty rates, the transparency and identity infrastructure, and admission of new regions.

Each layer's assembly is composed of delegates from the layer below, its vault is funded by scheduled contributions from the layer below, and its decisions bind only the scope it funds. The three refused departments are refused at every layer of the recursion, not only at the top.

## 4. The sporing protocol (how the fractal grows)

A mature cell seeds a new cell. The sequence:

1. **Eligibility.** The parent cell meets its template's §2.11 sporing criteria: sustained profitability, vault reserves above threshold, and at least one operator who has completed the parent's delegation ladder to GM standard.
2. **Proposal.** The candidate steward (per DC-MOGUL-001 Stage 3) files a launch proposal against a Validated or Proven template, localized for the target area, to the appropriate vault governance layer.
3. **Allocation.** Capital releases in milestone-gated stages from the cluster or regional expansion vault, with the parent cell's vault typically co-funding — the parent has skin in its spore.
4. **Launch.** The new cell launches at the template's current version, with the parent cell as designated mentor for the first year. Where the template invokes the Regulated Entity Pattern, the accountable licensed human is named before any capital releases.
5. **Inheritance.** The new cell inherits the full patch history of the template — every lesson from every prior instance — and begins contributing patches of its own per DC-TPL-000 §4.
6. **Independence.** Mentorship ends when the spore reaches Validated criteria independently; it is then eligible to spore in turn.

Growth is therefore paced by trained operators and vault cycles, not by capital raising — which is slower than venture-funded franchising and is intended to be.

## 5. Flow-through rails (everything moves on ecosystem infrastructure)

**Identity and credentials:** staff licences, trade certifications, training completions, and steward track records are W3C Verifiable Credentials on the ecosystem DID layer — portable across cells, auditable by regulators where applicable.

**Money:** all cell revenue, payroll, member dues, inter-cell settlement, vault contributions, and commons royalties flow through Lakou Protocol rails (DC-LKB-001…003), giving every layer real-time treasury visibility under CTS rules.

**Procurement:** cells buy ecosystem-first. Uniforms, linen, print, sensors, build-outs, beverage supply, and equipment route through internal supply lines as they come into existence — this is precisely where DC-VENTURE-001 §5's emergent ventures live: they are the fractal's internal supply chain, graduating from cell demand.

**Security and monitoring:** every cell's physical site is an OS-PACS install and NOC client by default; the security template line is thereby load-bearing for the whole fractal.

**Data:** sensor telemetry from cell sites feeds the mesh under CTS transparency and consent rules, compounding the network value curves of DC-SCALE-001.

**Tooling:** the agent cabinet operates as the shared back-office any cell rents — bookkeeping, compliance calendars, document generation — with the advisory/administrative boundary of DC-GOV-002 §2 enforced.

## 6. Franchise economics, co-op corrections

What is kept from franchising: a standardized, versioned operating system per venture type; brand and quality enforcement through published CTS metrics rather than inspections alone; a royalty per template §2.6 funding shared infrastructure.

What is corrected: the operator is a member-owner on the Principal Track, not a rent-paying licensee; the royalty flows to a commons the operator co-governs; template improvements flow bidirectionally (patches upstream, versions downstream) rather than top-down only; underperformance triggers mentorship and, ultimately, template-licence and vault-standing consequences through governance process — not unilateral termination; and the exit path is graduation to Principal with a spine of one's own, not acquisition by the franchisor.

## 7. Validation gate

Fractal architecture is a claim about replication, and one cell cannot validate a replication claim. The architecture in this document is considered structurally validated only when **N=2**: the first spore — operated by someone other than the founder, launched from a Validated template, financed through a completed vault allocation cycle — independently reaches Validated criteria. Until then, this document is design, and the ecosystem's execution status remains governed by DC-STATUS-001's critical path: incorporation, first invoice, first client, first cell.
