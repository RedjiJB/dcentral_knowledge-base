# DC-VENTURE-001 — Venture Sequencing Registry (Track 1: Security-Trades Spine)

**Registry ID:** DC-VENTURE-001
**Status:** Design Complete — Phase 0 in execution planning
**Owner:** Company Zero (holding layer)
**Related:** DC-LIC-001, DC-MSOC-001, DC-GOV-002, DC-MOGUL-001, DC-SHI-SPEC-001, DC-TM-POC-001, DC-STATUS-001

---

## 1. Purpose

This document sequences the principal's venture portfolio from first revenue to conglomerate maturity, binds every launch to funding and synergy conditions, and fixes the insertion points for D-Central infrastructure (sensor hardware, SHI nodes, mesh, monitoring). It is the reference implementation of what DC-MOGUL-001 later generalizes as Track 1: the Security-Trades Spine.

## 2. The three tests

No venture launches unless it passes all three tests. A concept failing any test is registered as a document and parked.

**Test 1 — Funding:** an existing operating business funds the launch. No venture is founded cold on external debt or personal guarantee alone.

**Test 2 — Integration:** an existing business supplies it or consumes from it (vertical), or shares its customer base (horizontal).

**Test 3 — Infrastructure:** the venture hosts or advances D-Central infrastructure — sensor nodes, mesh coverage, monitoring footprint, or a cooperative membership cell.

## 3. Phase sequence

### Phase 0 — Foundation (now → April 2027)

Company Zero incorporates federally as the holding company and IP owner. Only OpenSecure operates: guard subcontracting under the principal's individual licence, then OS-PACS technical installations. The NOC service tier (device-health monitoring per site) is added to the OS-PACS offer sheet from the first install, raising recurring revenue per client at zero capital cost. Seasonal construction and paving work continues as field intelligence and trade-network development for Phase 1. The SR&ED ledger runs from day one; the TrafficMesh POC (DC-TM-POC-001) proceeds on its one-Pi budget and quietly doubles as SHI-relevant R&D. Everything else remains registry documents. Revenue target per DC-STATUS-001 critical path: $1,180–$1,680/month by Month 5–6.

### Phase 1 — Security-construction spine (2027–2029)

Three vertical moves inside one customer base (commercial property owners): the OpenSecure agency licence (trigger and process per DC-LIC-001) enabling direct guard contracts and mobile patrol; the installation arm broadening from OS-PACS into general low-voltage and small construction/renovation subcontracts, leveraging the trade network; and the NOC tier maturing into MSSP-lite for the same clients. Dispatchable alarm monitoring is delivered via ULC-listed central-station partnership, with the NOC, analytics, and response-coordination margin retained in-house. The Tier A fly-away kit (DC-MSOC-001) is acquired in this phase once its trigger is met. First D-Central sensor hardware deploys into managed commercial sites at Month 9–12 — these are SHI-equivalent nodes in commercial form, field-validating the platform in sites OpenSecure is already paid to maintain.

### Phase 2 — First community asset: the gym (2029–2031)

The MMA/boxing gym is the first consumer venue, chosen for founder credibility (twice-daily training, Kali club involvement, coaching capability). The construction arm performs the build-out; OpenSecure secures and monitors the site; the gym launches as D-Central's first membership cooperative cell — the Lakou service layer made physical — with youth programs anchoring community legitimacy. This is the first venture to receive the DAO governance wrapper described in DC-GOV-002, applied only after the business is profitable and operationally stable.

### Phase 3 — Social hub (2031–2034)

Social club before nightclub: lower capital, membership-based cooperative structure, simpler licensing, and a physical venue for D-Central networking and governance assemblies. AGCO liquor licensing follows the Regulated Entity Pattern (DC-LIC-001 §4) with a personally accountable licence holder. OpenSecure staffs the doors and an event-security division emerges from that work; the construction arm performs the build-out. The nightclub is a later high-margin bolt-on once hospitality operating experience exists. The Tier B trailer (DC-MSOC-001) becomes acquirable in this window on OS-PACS Stage 2 recurring revenue.

### Phase 4 — Property layer (2032+)

A property holding company under Company Zero acquires the buildings housing the gym, club, and offices. Rent flows internally, assets appreciate on the balance sheet, and D-Central infrastructure — SHI nodes, mesh, TrafficMesh sensing — deploys in sovereign territory without client permission. The converged physical/cyber SOC (Year 3+ per DC-LIC-001 §5) becomes rational here, as does eventual consideration of an in-house listed central station.

## 4. SHI node insertion timeline

The SHI node is deliberately late — destination, not wedge. Now → Month 6: specification (DC-SHI-SPEC-001) and SR&ED ledger only, with the TrafficMesh Pi platform accumulating transferable R&D. Month 9–12: commercial-form nodes in OS-PACS managed sites as field validation. Year 2: first true residential node — the principal's own residence as node zero, then a small pilot cohort of community early adopters. Year 3+: scaling toward the 10³ operational break-even threshold from DC-SCALE-001, with the 10⁴ IESO VPP qualification as the long-horizon marker. The unifying logic: OS-PACS clients are the landing sites for SHI hardware, so scaling rides on security revenue rather than consumer sales from a standing start.

## 5. Emergent ventures (not founded — grown)

The following are registered as expected emergent lines that graduate from operations when volume justifies them, never founded cold: armored/valuables transport (from mobile patrol), uniform and linen service (from guards + gym + club), event production and promotion (from club + event security), valet operations (from club + patrol staffing), and beverage distribution (from club purchasing volume). Each remains subject to the three tests at graduation time.

## 6. Delegation ladder

Every venture follows the same operator progression: principal operates → supervisor hired → general manager hired with profit-share or equity earn-in → DAO governance tooling layered on top (community-facing ventures only) once profitable and boring. Governance automation is the last addition to any business, never the first. Regulated ventures stop at the GM stage and never receive operational DAO governance, per DC-LIC-001 §4.
