# DC-MOGUL-001 — The Principal Track Protocol

**Registry ID:** DC-MOGUL-001
**Status:** Design Complete — activates progressively; first cohort gated on Track 1 proof (DC-VENTURE-001 Phase 2+)
**Owner:** Company Zero as Protocol Steward / Node Zero
**Related:** DC-VENTURE-001 (Track 1 reference implementation), DC-GOV-002, DC-LIC-001, DC-LKB-001…003, Cooperative Transparency Stack, W3C DID/VC identity layer
**Supersedes in part:** DC-GOV-002's framing of the founder as sole conglomerate owner. The founder is reframed as first principal and protocol author; all DC-GOV-002 mechanics remain in force.

---

## 1. Purpose

The Principal Track Protocol generalizes the founder's venture path into a replicable ladder: anyone may become a multi-venture principal ("mogul") through the D-Central ecosystem, under full DAO governance except where statute requires an accountable human. The founder's Security-Trades Spine becomes the reference implementation rather than the sole instance. This is the Lakou principle applied to enterprise: the compound raises builders, not one patriarch.

## 2. Venture Templates

Every proven venture is documented as a replicable template in the registry. A template's required schema: capital requirement and staging plan; complete licensing and regulatory checklist (with the Regulated Entity Pattern applied where triggered); delegation ladder with hiring milestones; vault contribution structure; GM compensation model; DAO governance configuration (or explicit exclusion for regulated operations); and shared-infrastructure hooks — OpenSecure-lineage security services secure it, D-Central nodes deploy in it, and CTS opens its books.

Templates aggregate into **Spines** — proven sequences of mutually funding ventures. Track 1 (Security-Trades: security services → construction → gym → social venue → property) is the reference. Future spines emerge from other principals' domain expertise: a Hospitality Spine, a Logistics Spine, and so on. Each new spine, once proven, is documented back into the registry by its principal as a condition of protocol participation.

## 3. The Principal Track (progression ladder)

**Stage 1 — Member.** Joins a cooperative cell: gym member, guard, tradesperson, venue member. Establishes an ecosystem identity via the DID/VC layer.

**Stage 2 — Operator.** Takes an operating role — supervisor or GM — inside an existing venture. Execution is proven on transparent CTS metrics: the track record is visible, auditable, and portable. Ecosystem track record replaces conventional gatekeeping (inherited capital, bank approval) as the qualification for advancement.

**Stage 3 — Steward.** Leads a venture launch from an existing template, financed through the vault process (§4), as accountable operator and, where statute requires, licensed principal of the regulated wrapper.

**Stage 4 — Principal.** Runs a spine of ventures under the protocol, with the DC-GOV-002 governance architecture and time model applied to their own portfolio. A principal at this stage is expected to contribute at least one new or refined template back to the commons.

Advancement between stages is granted by vault governance vote on the basis of published metrics, not by the founder's personal discretion. The founder holds no permanent veto beyond the protocol steward duties in §6.

## 4. Financing — the venture studio vault

The expansion vault (DC-GOV-002 §2, Layer 4) generalizes into a venture studio fund. Aspiring stewards pitch template-based launches to a vault governance vote; capital releases in milestone-gated stages; repayment flows back as vault contributions from the funded venture, financing the next steward. Each cooperative cell's community vault may additionally seed its own members' progressions through Stages 1–2 (training, licensing courses, certification costs).

The commons sustains itself through a small equity position or revenue royalty held by the protocol — not by Company Zero personally — in every vault-financed venture.

**Risk architecture (non-negotiable):** the vault maintains a loss reserve sized against expected venture failure rates; allocation follows portfolio logic across stewards, never concentration in one launch; capital staging means a failing venture loses its next tranche, not the vault's principal. The protocol promises access to the ladder, never moguldom. Templates, transparency, the apprenticeship stages, and staged capital are failure-rate reducers, not eliminators, and every cohort agreement states this in plain language.

## 5. The regulated boundary, generalized

The Regulated Entity Pattern (DC-LIC-001 §4) is a first-class protocol pattern applied by every spine: any statutorily accountable activity — security agencies, liquor licences, licensed trades, financial services, childcare, health services — operates inside a conventional corporate wrapper with a named, licensed, personally liable principal drawn from the steward or principal ranks. The DAO decides whether to fund and who the accountable human is; it never governs regulated operations. Full DAO governance applies everywhere else.

## 6. Company Zero as Protocol Steward

At protocol maturity, Company Zero's role is: maintain the registry and template library; operate shared services — the agent cabinet becomes a shared back-office (bookkeeping, compliance calendars, document generation) available to any principal; host and maintain the transparency infrastructure (CTS) and identity layer; administer the vault tooling; and earn published steward fees for these functions. The founder's personal wealth derives from his own spine of ventures plus the appreciation of the commons he authored — the family-office endgame in which the family is the membership.

## 7. Activation gates

The protocol does not admit external stewards until Track 1 has proven at minimum: one profitable regulated venture (OpenSecure with agency licence), one profitable DAO-wrapped community venture (the gym as first co-op cell), and one completed vault-financed capital cycle. Opening the ladder before the reference implementation works would export an unproven model and drain the commons — the same failure mode DC-STATUS-001 warns against, at protocol scale.
