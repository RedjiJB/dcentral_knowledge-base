### Level Progression System

#### Apprentice Level (Entry)
- **Requirements**: Complete basic safety training
- **Capabilities**: Supervised operations only
- **Earning Potential**: $10-20/hour
- **Supervision**: Required for all activities
- **Duration**: Typically 2-4 weeks

#### Practitioner Level (Standard)
- **Requirements**: Earn 3 skill badges + 1 capability certificate
- **Capabilities**: Independent basic operations
- **Earning Potential**: $20-35/hour
- **Supervision**: Required for complex tasks only
- **Duration**: Typically 2-6 months

#### Specialist Level (Advanced)
- **Requirements**: Earn 6 skill badges + 2 capability certificates + 1 specialty endorsement
- **Capabilities**: Independent complex operations and basic supervision
- **Earning Potential**: $35-50/hour
- **Supervision**: Can supervise apprentice-level participants
- **Duration**: Typically 6-18 months
