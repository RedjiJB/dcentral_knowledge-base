# Integrated Community Sovereignty Platform: 16-Sector Critical Infrastructure Integration

## Executive Summary: Complete Community Self-Sufficiency

Building on the proven **School-as-a-Micro-DC** foundation, this enhanced framework creates a **Community Sovereignty Platform** that integrates all 16 U.S.-defined critical infrastructure sectors into a single, containerized micro-data center that can sustain a community of 500-2,000 people through any crisis while maintaining global connectivity and standards compliance.
