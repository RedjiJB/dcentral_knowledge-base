# Rezilyon Digital: Enhanced Haitian Community Mesh Network
*A Community-Owned Digital Sovereignty Platform with Diaspora Skills Bridge*

## Executive Summary

This enhanced plan outlines the implementation of a community-owned mesh network federation in Haiti that builds independent infrastructure while creating bridges to diaspora expertise for technical capacity building. Recognizing Haiti's unique position outside North American regulatory frameworks and the urgent need for transparent, corruption-resistant systems, this model uses blockchain technology to ensure accountability while building genuine community wealth.

**Key Innovation**: Diaspora skills bridge program where Haitian professionals abroad provide remote training, technical support, and investment while community-owned infrastructure creates local economic opportunities through transparent blockchain-based systems that resist corruption and political capture.

**Realistic Economic Model**: $1.8M initial investment for 2,500-household regional network, achieving operational sustainability by month 30 (development funding timeline), generating $200,000-600,000 annual revenue through proven local transaction fees and diaspora service exports.
