## §2.1: CISCO CERTIFICATION STRUCTURE

**Cisco has reorganized its credential portfolio multiple times. As of August 2026:**

### Career Path: CCST Foundation → Associate → CCNP (8 specializations) → CCIE → CCDE → Expert/Business

**4 Foundation (new, 2023+):**
- Cisco Certified Support Technician (CCST) IT Fundamentals (007-1001)
- CCST Networking (007-1002)
- CCST Cybersecurity (007-1003)
- Cisco Certified Technician (CCT) Field Technician (800-150)

**3 Associate:**
- CCNA (200-301) — prerequisites: none
- CCNA Cybersecurity Operations (200-201) — prerequisites: none (specialist track)
- CCNA Enterprise Automation (200-901) — prerequisites: none (specialist track)

**CCNP Tracks (8 specializations, each ~5 certs, ~35 total after 2023 restructuring):**
1. **Enterprise** (ENCOR 350-401 + SCOR 350-701)
2. **Data Center** (DCCOR 350-601 + spec)
3. **Service Provider** (SPCOR 350-501 + spec)
4. **Collaboration** (CLCOR 350-801 + spec)
5. **Wireless** (WLCOR 350-101 + spec)
6. **Security** (SCOR 350-701) — overlaps with Enterprise
7. **Cybersecurity** (CBRCOR 350-201 + spec)
8. **Automation** (AUTOCOR 350-901 + spec)

**Each CCNP track includes:**
- 1 Core exam (e.g., ENCOR 350-401)
- 1 Concentration exam (e.g., SCOR 350-701 for Enterprise)
- Specialist credentials (5–8 Specialist-level exams per track, optional for CCNP but required for deeper expertise)

**CCIE Tracks (6, retired CCNP specializations now map to CCIE specialties):**
1. CCIE Enterprise Infrastructure (written 350-901, lab varies)
2. CCIE Data Center (350-601, lab)
3. CCIE Service Provider (350-501, lab)
4. CCIE Collaboration (350-801, lab)
5. CCIE Wireless (350-101, lab)
6. CCIE Security (350-701, lab)

**CCDE (Cisco Certified Design Expert):**
- CCDE 400-007 (design-focused, different from CCIE)
- Prerequisites: CCNP-level or higher in at least one specialization

**Technical & Business Certifications:**
- Cisco AI Technical Practitioner (810-110)
- Cisco AI Business Practitioner
- Meraki Solutions Specialist
- Cisco Cloud Security Manager
- Cisco Systems Manager

## §2.10: CISCO PREREQUISITES & RENEWAL

**Renewal Strategy:**

- **CCST:** 2 years; renewed by earning 120 Cisco U continuing education credits OR passing a CCST or higher exam
- **CCNA:** 3 years; renewed by 120 CE credits or passing CCNA/CCNP/CCIE written
- **CCNP:** 3 years; renewed by 120 CE credits or passing a CCNP core exam OR 1 Expert-level (CCIE) exam
- **CCIE:** 3 years; renewed by 120 CE credits or passing another Expert-level exam
- **CCDE:** 3 years; renewed by 120 CE credits or passing CCDE again

**Important Rule (as of Aug 2026):**
> Any Expert-level exam (CCIE written or CCDE) OR 120 Cisco U CE credits earned in any year renews ALL active Cisco certifications simultaneously for that year.

This means once at CCIE level, all lower certs (CCNA, CCNP, etc.) stay active if you maintain Expert status.
