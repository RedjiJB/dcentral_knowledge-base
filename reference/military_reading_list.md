> **Scope and honesty note:** these lists draw from published military doctrine, professional military education (PME) reading lists (US Army/Marine Corps/Navy/Air Force Chief of Staff reading lists, Canadian Armed Forces professional development reading, NATO doctrine), classic strategic theory, and reputable technical/field manuals. The commercial books around them provide context, theory, and depth doctrine alone doesn't cover.
