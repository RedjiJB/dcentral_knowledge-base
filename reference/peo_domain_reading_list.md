# PEO DOMAIN READING LIST — EXPANDED (12 BOOKS PER DOMAIN)
## Redji Jean Baptiste (Toussaint) — Engineering Fundamentals Study Plan
### For the non-accredited-degree / academic-requirements exam route to P.Eng, if pursued

---

> **Is this from an official PEO reading list? No.** PEO does not publish a reading list. This is a self-built study plan mapping the actual knowledge areas PEO's academic assessors evaluate — for the confirmatory-exam route taken by candidates without a CEAB-accredited degree — to real engineering textbooks. Treat it as a designed curriculum, not an official document.
>
> **Is there a real PEO Economics domain? Yes.** Engineering Economics is a genuine, named subject in PEO's confirmatory academic-requirements exam list. It is distinct from the **Professional Practice Exam (PPE)** — the mandatory ethics/law/conduct exam every P.Eng candidate takes regardless of route. The previous version of this list merged those two into one domain; that understated the distinction. They are now split into Domain 9 (Economics) and Domain 10 (Professional Practice), which is the accurate structure.
>
> **Status:** background/optional. P.Eng is not currently a required goal in the timeline. This exists as insurance if that changes, and because the domain coverage has standalone value regardless of licensure.

## Domain 10 — Professional Practice, Ethics & Law
> This is the mandatory Professional Practice Exam (PPE) content area — required of every P.Eng candidate, not just the exam-route path. PEO's own PPE study guide and the Ontario *Professional Engineers Act* and regulations are the primary, authoritative preparation materials — the texts below are supplementary depth reading, and confidence on availability/edition is lower here than the other nine domains, so verify before purchasing.
1. *Canadian Professional Engineering and Geoscience: Practice and Ethics* — Andrews & Kocaoglu (the closest thing to a Canada-specific standard text)
2. *Engineering Ethics: Concepts and Cases* — Harris, Pritchard, Rabins, James & Englehardt
3. *Ethics in Engineering* — Mike Martin & Roland Schinzinger
4. *To Engineer Is Human: The Role of Failure in Successful Design* — Henry Petroski (case-study foundation for engineering judgment and liability)
5. The Ontario **Professional Engineers Act, R.S.O. 1990** and O. Reg. 941/90 — primary legal source, not a commercial book, read directly
6. PEO's own **Professional Practice Exam (PPE) Guidelines and Study Materials** — the authoritative exam-prep source, available directly from PEO
7. *Business Law: Text and Cases* — Clarkson, Miller & Cross (Canadian edition) — general contract/business law foundation
8. *Canadian Construction Law* — general Canadian construction-law reference (verify current edition/author before purchase)
9. *Intellectual Property Law for Engineers and Scientists* — Howard Bruck (general IP grounding relevant to patents/licensing)
10. *Introduction to Environmental Engineering and Science* — Masters & Ela (regulatory/environmental-law touchpoint)
11. *Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK Guide)* — PMI (procedural/professional-conduct overlap with engineering project delivery)
12. Provincial Occupational Health and Safety legislation (Ontario OHSA) — primary legal source, read directly rather than via a commercial text
