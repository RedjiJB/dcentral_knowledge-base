## Current understanding

Two federal CCPC incorporation filings implementing a deliberate IP-holding-vs-operating split: CivicMesh
Inc. holds platform IP and licenses it out; TrafficMesh Technologies Inc. is one of the operating
entities that receives an IP sublicence from CivicMesh Inc. and actually runs deployments. Both are
100%-owned by D-Central Group Inc., and the entities' stated scopes are mutually exclusive rather than
overlapping.

**CivicMesh Inc. (incorporated):** holds all CivicMesh platform IP (architecture, federation protocol,
privacy architecture, DID/VC schema registry, NOC dashboard, all five module codebases), earns
platform-fee/data-marketplace/licensing revenue, and explicitly does NOT operate direct municipal
enforcement, camera deployments, or security response — those are named as TrafficMesh Technologies
Inc., CommunityShield Inc., and D-Central Guard Inc. respectively [LEGAL-CM-001 §1]. Five SR&ED-eligible
R&D activities are named (federation protocol design, DID/VC schema development, on-chain anchoring,
privacy architecture, insurance data-product development) [§2]. Ten executed/planned contracts are
listed, including IP sublicences to both TrafficMesh Technologies Inc. and CommunityShield Inc. [§3].

**TrafficMesh Technologies Inc. (incorporated):** the operating entity for TrafficMesh deployments,
receiving its IP rights via intercompany sublicence from CivicMesh Inc. (consistent with CivicMesh
Inc.'s own stated sublicence-out arrangement — no conflict). It contracts directly with OC Transpo and
the City of Ottawa, employs the technical team, and is the entity that signs the OC Transpo pilot
agreement [LEGAL-TM-001 §1]. A distinct set of five SR&ED activities is named (edge AI violation
detection, ALPR optimization, OBD-II telemetry fusion, digital twin mapping, chain-of-custody
pipeline) — operational/deployment-focused, complementary to rather than overlapping with CivicMesh
Inc.'s platform-layer SR&ED list. An IRAP salary-cost table gives specific eligible-role cost splits
(founder: $70-80K salary, IRAP covers 80% = $56-64K, founder nets $14-16K) [§2]. Five key operational
documents are cross-referenced [§3].
