# DC-CM-PARTNER-INCENTIVES-RECONCILED-001 — CivicMesh Partner & Government Incentive Programmes, Consolidated (v1, generated 2026-09-05)

Per [DC-CONSOLIDATOR-STD-001](../standards/DC-CONSOLIDATOR-STD-001.md), topic `civicmesh-partner-incentive-programmes` (2 docs).

## Current understanding

Two incentive briefs for two different partner classes — government (MKT-CM-GOV-003) and MSSP
operators (MKT-MSSP-001) — not tightly cross-referenced but non-overlapping in scope.

**Government incentive stack (incorporated):** municipalities get enforcement revenue without capital
cost, road-condition data, reduced police-dispatch costs, and community goodwill, in exchange for a
self-funding hardware-rebate and node-owner-stipend programme explicitly argued to have zero net
municipal cost since it's funded from the enforcement revenue it generates [MKT-CM-GOV-003 §1]. Three
named provincial/federal incentive programmes (Ontario Smart Communities, Ontario Municipal
Cybersecurity, Federal Smart Cities Challenge) are identified as access points, each requiring the City
of Ottawa or a similar municipality as the formal applicant with CivicMesh named as technology partner
[MKT-CM-GOV-003 §2]. Insurance-incentive integration spans HOA/condo communities (premium reduction
after a 90-day coverage-density certification), individual opted-in residents, and commercial fleet
operators [MKT-CM-GOV-003 §3].

**MSSP partner programme (incorporated):** the addressable Canadian market is sized at 5,000+
municipalities, 35,000+ condo corporations, 15,000+ HOAs, and 3,000+ campuses/business parks
[MKT-MSSP-001 §1]. MSSPs earn three recurring revenue streams (platform fee, per-node management fee,
citation-revenue share) averaging **8-12% margin on gross citation revenue plus $5-25/node/month
management fee** [MKT-MSSP-001 §2] — this figure matches FIN-CM-005's independently-stated MSSP margin
range exactly, a consistency check rather than a conflict. Five client-type service packages are
priced by monthly revenue range and typical sales-cycle length, from HOA/condo (2-8 weeks, $500-3,000/
month) up to full municipal fleet (12-24 months, $30,000-100,000/month) [MKT-MSSP-001 §3]. A seven-step
certification path runs from Track 2 staff certification through Bronze credentialing on Polygon to
Silver/Gold growth over Year 1-2 [MKT-MSSP-001 §4].
