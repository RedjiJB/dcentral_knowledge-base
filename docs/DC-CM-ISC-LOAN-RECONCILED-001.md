# DC-CM-ISC-LOAN-RECONCILED-001 — CivicMesh ISC Grant Application Framework, Consolidated (v1, generated 2026-09-05)

Per [DC-CONSOLIDATOR-STD-001](../standards/DC-CONSOLIDATOR-STD-001.md), topic `civicmesh-isc-loan-application` (2 docs).

## Current understanding

A sequential two-phase Innovative Solutions Canada (ISC) grant application plan: LOAN-CM-001 covers
Phase 1 (up to $150,000 for a 6-month PoC), LOAN-CM-002 covers Phase 2 (up to $1,000,000 for a 2-year
full build), explicitly gated on Phase 1 success. Both are draft frameworks, not yet-submittable
applications — LOAN-CM-001 cannot be filed until a matching challenge is posted on the ISC board;
LOAN-CM-002 is meant to be completed only after Phase 1 results exist.

**Phase 1 framework (incorporated):** a challenge watch-list across five federal departments (Public
Safety Canada, Transport Canada, RCMP, National Defence dual-use stream, Infrastructure Canada) with
department-specific fit rationale and alert keywords; a worked Transport Canada road-safety PoC scope
naming five specific technical deliverables; an application-narrative template citing a specific
factual claim (Ontario's Nov 2025 automated-speed-enforcement ban eliminating $38.7M/yr in Ottawa
enforcement revenue) as the problem-statement hook; and explicit compatibility with IRAP and SR&ED as
complementary, stackable funding (ISC counts as government assistance and reduces SR&ED-eligible
expenditure, but all three can run concurrently) [LOAN-CM-001 §1-4].

**Phase 2 framework (incorporated):** six named readiness criteria required before applying (formal
departmental Phase 1 acceptance, an operational OC Transpo pilot, ≥3 months of real citation/revenue
data, a completed PIPEDA PIA with zero privacy complaints, GitHub/academic community-engagement
evidence, and 2+ additional municipalities in active negotiation); a five-line build-scope budget
allocation across all CivicMesh modules, DID/VC infrastructure, the nine-application suite, MSSP
certification, and national-expansion groundwork; and a commercialization plan tracing a specific
procurement path (OC Transpo → Ottawa fleet → Hamilton/Mississauga/Toronto → provincial framework)
[LOAN-CM-002 §1-3].

**Sequencing (incorporated):** LOAN-CM-002's readiness criteria explicitly cite LOAN-CM-001 and its
downstream artifacts (DEPLOY-TM-001, REG-CM-001, MKT-TM-GOV-002) as the evidence base Phase 2 depends
on — a stated dependency, not an independent claim.
