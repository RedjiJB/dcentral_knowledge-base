## Current understanding

Two distinct Mitacs Accelerate application tracks, both gated on the same incubator-acceptance
prerequisite and explicitly cross-referenced: LOAN-CM-003 funds university research internships;
LOAN-CM-004 funds a founder stipend for the same founder's own commercialization work.

**University research track (incorporated):** business contributes $7,500 per 4-6 month internship
unit, Mitacs covers up to $15,000 (75% of total) — net $7,500 for $22,500 of research labour. Five
specific proposed research questions are named across four institutions (Carleton computer vision,
uOttawa engineering/edge computing, uOttawa law/VC-evidence-admissibility, Algonquin hardware, and a
traffic-engineering deterrence study), each with a named target publication venue
[LOAN-CM-003 §1]. A five-point "what makes faculty say yes" rationale (genuine publication-worthy
research question, data/access, student training value, no IP conflict since CivicMesh is open source,
low administrative burden) and a 7-step application process are given [LOAN-CM-003 §2-3].

**Founder stipend track (incorporated):** up to $7,500 per internship unit as a founder living-cost
stipend, explicitly framed as complementing IRAP salary reimbursements rather than overlapping with
them. Eligibility is confirmed criterion-by-criterion against the specific founder's status (enrolled
Algonquin student, startup based on coursework-era R&D, incubator affiliation pending)
[LOAN-CM-004 §1]. The proposed internship work plan names five concrete commercialization activities
(OC Transpo pilot proposal, HTA regulatory-compliance opinion, first HOA client acquisition, IRAP/SR&ED
documentation setup, and university-partnership outreach) — the last of these explicitly *is* the
LOAN-CM-003 research-partnership track, making LOAN-CM-004 partly a funding vehicle for pursuing
LOAN-CM-003 [LOAN-CM-004 §2].

**Shared incubator requirement (incorporated):** both applications require housing in or affiliation
with an approved Mitacs incubator. Both name the same three targets in the same priority order
(Algonquin College Innovation Hub first — easiest given the founder's current enrollment there, Invest
Ottawa LaunchPad in parallel, Carleton Sprott once the Carleton research relationship exists), with
consistent monthly timing (Algonquin/Invest Ottawa Month 1-2, Carleton Month 3-4)
[LOAN-CM-003 §4, LOAN-CM-004 §3].
