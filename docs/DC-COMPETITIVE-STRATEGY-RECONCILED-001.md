**Recap from the prior consolidation (incorporated by reference):** DC-STRAT-005 established a
four-element moat (cooperative structure, open source, DID/VC credentialing, D-Credit) and named the
"replication paradox" — a private competitor cannot replicate D-Central's model without becoming a
cooperative, which eliminates the extractive model that makes it investable in the first place. INV-005
applied this to six named incumbents (Flock Safety, Nexar, ShotSpotter, Ring/Amazon, Motorola Vigilant,
Waze/Google) for investor due diligence, explicitly building on DC-STRAT-005's replication-paradox
framing rather than re-deriving it independently.

**INV-006's new contribution (incorporated):** reframes the same replication paradox for impact/ESG-
mandated investors rather than conventional VCs, opening with a broader indictment of "platform
economics" extraction generally (Uber/drivers, Airbnb/hosts, Ring/homeowners, Waze/drivers), arguing
extraction is inherently politically unstable while cooperative ownership is structurally stable because
the community advocates for continuation rather than against the platform [§1-2]. A six-row financial-
metrics comparison table (conventional platform vs. D-Central cooperative) argues the cooperative
structure is not a financial constraint but a competitive advantage across CAC, retention/churn,
regulatory risk, network-effect quality, brand trust, and moat durability [§3]. For the specific impact-
investor audience, it resolves the "financial return vs. mission alignment" tension by citing mission-
lock provisions (LEGAL-002) that bind the cooperative/open-source commitment through any change of
control, framing this as due-diligence de-risking rather than a constraint. A three-part return model is
given (equity appreciation at the holding-company level, data-marketplace revenue, MSSP platform fees),
explicitly noting cooperative distribution at the operating level does not eliminate equity value at the
holding-company level — and impact is to be measured via an annual transparency report (DC-STRAT-004),
not merely claimed [§4].
